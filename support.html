Send us a message on our feedback page. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

Common questions

How do I start a game?

Tap Create, name your game, pick the dates and challenges, then open the Manage tab to add teams and send the invite code.

How do my friends join?

They install SnapQuest, tap Join and type the 6-letter invite code — or tap the invite link you send them.

How do points work?

Posting proof is not enough! When a round ends, the other players vote Accept or Reject on each post. Only accepted posts score points. If the vote is tied, a coin flip decides. Pro games keep the leaderboard secret until the final 24 hours.

What does SnapQuest Pro include?

Free games have 2 players, up to 10 challenges and last 1 day (photos only). SnapQuest Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited players and challenges, games up to 1 year long, videos, the Blooper Reel, surprise bonus challenges, voting and badges — for everyone in every game you own.

I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Home screen → person icon → Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Someone posted something offensive. What can I do?

Tap ••• on the post → Report post or Block. The game owner sees reports in the Manage tab and can delete posts or remove players. You can also tell us on our feedback page and we will act within 24 hours.

How do I delete my photos or my data?

Delete any of your posts with ••• → Delete. To delete everything about you, send a request on our feedback page and we will remove it within 30 days. See our privacy policy.
