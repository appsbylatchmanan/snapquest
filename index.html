How it works

Step 1

Create a game

Name your game, pick the dates and choose your challenges – write your own or start from the ready-made packs.

Step 2

Invite your friends

Share the 6-letter invite code or link. Friends tap Join, type the code and they're in a team.

Step 3

Snap, vote, win

Post photo proof for each challenge. When a round ends, everyone votes Accept or Reject – accepted posts score points.

Made for friends, families and teams

Challenges in rounds

Split a long game into rounds. Future rounds stay secret until they start, so there's always something new.

Fair voting

Other players vote on every post – you can't judge your own or your team's. A tie is settled by a coin flip that everyone sees.

Leaderboard

Watch the scores climb. In Pro games the leaderboard stays secret until the final 24 hours, for a big reveal.

Surprise bonuses Pro

Drop a time-limited bonus challenge in the middle of the game to shake things up.

Blooper Reel Pro

The funniest fails get their own place, so the best laughs aren't lost.

Badges & best photo Pro

Vote for the best photo and earn badges along the way.

Private and safe

Invite-only games

Only players in a game can see its photos, videos and names. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. Privacy policy

Kind by design

Report a post or block a player in two taps. The game owner can remove posts and players, and we act on reports within 24 hours. Community rules

No account needed

Just type a display name to play. We don't ask for your email, phone number or a password.
