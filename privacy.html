SnapQuest is made by Ragoo Latchmanan in Brisbane, Australia ("we"). This policy explains what information the SnapQuest app collects and how we use it. We keep it simple: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your display name – the name you type when you start, shown to players in your games.
  • Photos, videos and sound – only the ones you choose to post as challenge proof.
  • Captions, votes, emoji reactions, teams and scores – the things you do in a game.
  • A random user ID – created automatically so the app knows which posts are yours. We do not ask for your email, phone number or password.
  • Reports – if you report a post, we store the reason and who reported it.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing history. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

Only to run the game: show your posts to the players in your game, count scores and votes, and keep the app safe (reports, blocking and removing players). Your information is not used for tracking or advertising.

Who can see it

Games are private and invite-only. Only players in a game can see that game's photos, videos and names. The game owner can see reports and remove posts or players.

Where it is stored

We use Google Firebase to store data securely. Game data is stored in Sydney, Australia. Photos and videos are stored in the United States. Data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and the servers.

Purchases

SnapQuest Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment details — we never see them.

How long we keep it & deleting your data

Your posts stay until you or the game owner delete them. You can delete your own posts at any time in the app. To delete all your data, email ray@latchmanan.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

SnapQuest is rated 13+. Younger children should only play with a parent or guardian managing the game.

Changes & contact

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Email ray@latchmanan.com. This policy follows the Australian Privacy Principles.
