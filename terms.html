Community Rules

Home Support Privacy Policy Community Rules

Last updated: 27 November 2026

SnapQuest is for fun with family, friends and workmates. By using SnapQuest you agree to these rules. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

  • No nudity, sexual, hateful, violent, bullying or illegal content.
  • Be kind. Don't post photos of strangers in a way that embarrasses them, and ask before posting other people.
  • Stay safe and follow the law while doing challenges. Skip any challenge that isn't safe for you.
  • Only post photos and videos you have the right to share.

Breaking the rules: posts can be reported and players can be blocked. Game owners can delete posts and remove players. We review reports sent to ray@latchmanan.com within 24 hours and will remove content and users who break these rules.

SnapQuest is provided under Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.
